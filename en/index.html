Once a year, even if you see well

We are a registered healthcare establishment. In the consulting room we measure your prescription and check the health of your eyes, which is how we catch things early, before you notice them.

  • Eye test

    We measure how well you see far and near, and check how both eyes work together.

  • Retinal photography

    A photo of the back of the eye, so we can keep an eye on your retina from one test to the next.

  • Contact lenses

    We fit your lenses and show you how to put them in and look after them.

  • Ortho-K

    Lenses you wear while you sleep that correct short-sightedness during the day, with no glasses.

  • Low vision

    Optical aids for people who no longer see well even with glasses: magnifiers, filters and telescopes.

Varifocals

See far, in between and close up with the same pair of glasses

Varifocals only work if they’re measured properly: the height of each pupil, the distance between your eyes, how the frame sits on your face. That’s why Essilor made us a Varilux specialist centre, and why we take those measurements with precision instruments.

Pardon?

When you can hear, but can’t make out the words

Hearing loss comes on little by little and nearly always in both ears, which is why we tend to notice it late. A full hearing assessment takes four painless tests.

Does any of this sound familiar?

Tick the ones that ring a bell.

  1. Video otoscopy

    We look at the ear canal and eardrum with a camera linked to a screen, and you see it with us.

  2. Pure-tone audiometry

    In a soundproof booth you listen to tones at different volumes, so we find your hearing threshold.

  3. Speech audiometry

    The same test with words, so we know how much of what you hear you actually understand.

  4. Discomfort level

    We find the volume at which sounds start to bother you, so your hearing aid never goes that far.

With the results we explain which hearing aid suits you, in-the-ear or behind-the-ear, and fit it to your hearing. How a hearing aid works →

What people ask us most at the counter

How often should I have my eyes tested?

The World Health Organization recommends an eye test once a year, even if you don’t wear glasses. It keeps your prescription up to date and helps catch problems such as cataracts early.

Do you do hearing tests and hearing aids?

Yes. We do video otoscopy, pure-tone audiometry in a soundproof booth and speech audiometry, and we fit digital hearing aids, both in-the-ear and behind-the-ear. Call your nearest shop to book.

What does being a Varilux specialist centre mean?

It’s a title Essilor, the maker of Varilux lenses, gives to opticians with precise measuring equipment and staff trained to fit varifocals. We have held it since 2000.

Do I need an appointment for an eye test?

It’s better. That way we can see you without rushing and with the consulting room free. Call Albox on +34 950 43 00 12 or Huércal-Overa on +34 950 13 41 99.

Do you fit contact lenses?

Yes. We fit contact lenses and also Ortho-K, lenses you wear overnight that correct short-sightedness during the day.
