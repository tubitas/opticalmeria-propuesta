Hearing tests and hearing aids in Albox and Huércal-Overa

Hearing loss comes on slowly and nearly always in both ears. The sooner it’s picked up, the more sounds you get back.

  • Types of hearing loss

Why hearing goes

Hearing loss is difficulty hearing certain sounds. Because it creeps up gradually, we often only notice it once it’s significant. The most common causes are:

  • Age.
  • Family history.
  • Spending a lot of time around loud noise.
  • Infections and some medicines.
  • Knocks and accidents.

Types of hearing loss

Depending on how much is lost, hearing loss is mild, moderate, severe or profound. Depending on where the problem lies:

Conductive

Sound doesn’t reach the receptors of the inner ear properly. It affects all pitches equally and a good part of the hearing can usually be recovered.

Sensorineural

The receptors are damaged. It doesn’t affect all pitches equally, and the typical complaint is “I can hear, but I can’t understand”.

Mixed

Both together: sound isn’t conducted properly and the receptors are damaged too.

The tests we do

  • Video otoscopy. We look at the ear canal and eardrum with an otoscope linked to a screen.
  • Pure-tone audiometry. In a soundproof booth, with tones at different volumes, by air and bone conduction.
  • Speech audiometry. The same, but with words, to find out how much you understand.
  • Discomfort level. The volume at which sounds start to bother you.

Types of hearing aid

All the hearing aids we fit are digital: smaller, more powerful and adjustable to your hearing. The type depends on your hearing assessment.

In the ear

They sit in the ear canal and the visible part of the ear, custom-made from an impression we take in the consulting room. The smallest, CIC, are completely hidden.

Behind the ear

A small case behind the ear connected by a thin tube to an earpiece inside the ear, with a custom or standard mould. They are usually more powerful.
