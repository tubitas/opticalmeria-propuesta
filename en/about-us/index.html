A journey through time, and friendship

We’ve seen in a new century, lived through the technology revolution and globalisation, watched more fashions come and go than we can count, and all of it alongside you. Over the years we’ve changed our look, but we still value what really matters: looking after your eyes with the highest quality and the latest technical advances.

Our philosophy has always been the same: give you the best solution at the best price, with a selection of exclusive brands and new lens and frame designs for every need, whether it’s about design or fashion.

Varilux specialist centre since 2000

It’s a title Essilor awards to opticians who give the best service and care and work with the latest advances. To hold it, our shops have the most precise measuring instruments on the market and highly qualified staff.

We are a healthcare establishment

Our work is about your health, both your sight and your hearing. That’s why every test is done by a qualified professional, taking all the time it needs.

Call whichever shop is closer to you. We speak English

We’ll give you a time for your eye test or hearing test. If you’re not sure what you need, tell us on the phone and we’ll point you in the right direction.
